About Us

Our Story

The Nimba Community Western Australia was established to bring together people of Nimba origin and descent, along with our families and friends, who have made Western Australia our home. Far from our beloved Nimba County in Liberia, we found that our greatest strength lies in staying connected — to our culture, to our people, and to each other.


We are a voluntary, non-political, non-profit cultural and social association. Our members live across WA, coming together in fellowship, celebration, and mutual support. Whether we are celebrating our heritage through cultural events, supporting one another through difficult times, or contributing back to our communities in Australia and Nimba — we remain true to the values that define us.

Our Purpose & Objectives

  • ✅ Unite people of Nimba origin, their descendants, families and friends in fellowship and mutual support
  • ✅ Preserve, promote and celebrate the cultural heritage, languages and traditions of Nimba
  • ✅ Operate our Welfare System to provide practical and financial support to members in times of loss or celebration
  • ✅ Foster cooperation and friendship with other Liberian, African and multicultural communities in WA
  • ✅ Support charitable and development initiatives in Nimba County, Liberia
  • ✅ Promote the welfare, dignity and advancement of our members within the Australian community

Nimba Community Welfare System

Our Welfare System is the heart of our community — a mutual support fund where every member contributes a small amount regularly, so that when any member faces loss or celebrates a major milestone, the whole community stands with them.

How It Works

  • Contribution: Each participating member contributes $20–$25 AUD per fortnight (exact amount confirmed at the Adoption Meeting).
  • Waiting Period: Benefits become available after 3 months from the date of registration and first contribution.
  • Eligibility: You must be a financial Ordinary Member of the Community and remain up to date with contributions.
  • Voluntary: Membership in the Welfare System is voluntary — you may join the Community without joining the Welfare System.

Benefits at a Glance

EventMaximum Benefit Payable
Death of a participating memberUp to $5,000 AUD
Death of an immediate family member of a participantUp to $5,000 AUD
Graduation of a participating memberUp to $2,000 AUD
Graduation of an immediate family memberUp to $2,000 AUD

* Immediate family: spouse/partner, children, parents, siblings. Other relationships considered at the Committee's discretion. Benefits are maximum amounts and are subject to Fund capacity, verification, and compliance with Policy.

How to Make a Claim

  1. Download and complete the Welfare Claim Form below
  2. Attach supporting documentation (death certificate, funeral notice, graduation certificate)
  3. Submit to the Welfare Chairperson in person or via email
  4. Your claim will be verified and reviewed within 14 days
  5. Payment is made by direct bank transfer upon approval — signed by two authorised officers

Culture & Events

Our Culture

Nimba County is home to diverse ethnic groups including the Dan, Mano, Kpelle, Loma, and many others — each with rich languages, traditions, music, dance, and storytelling. Here in WA, we keep our culture alive through our gatherings, celebrations, and the way we support one another.

Our Cultural Officer organises events that celebrate our heritage — from traditional ceremonies and cultural days to music, dance, and sharing our stories with the younger generation born here in Australia.
